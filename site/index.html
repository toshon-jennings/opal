The story

From a fork nobody would bet on

Perci started life as a fork of a rough open-source take on Claude Desktop. The chat window survived. Almost nothing else did. Tool by tool, window by window, it grew into the thing its builder actually wanted: one place where the whole working day happens.

It’s still open source, still local-first, and still growing.

Forked from Open-claude by Damien Chakma · Integrates OpenClaw

Free. Not “free for now.”

Perci, as it exists today, is free and open source — and it will stay that way. Somewhere down the road there may be a Perci Pro subscription with expanded features. But everything you see here stays free. No trial clocks, no rug pulls, no features quietly moving behind a paywall.

Pro, if it happens, will be additive — for people who want more, not a toll on people who don’t.

Keys never leave

Encrypted with the OS keychain via Electron safeStorage. They’re never sent to a server.

Local models, first-class

Ollama, LM Studio, and vLLM sit beside the cloud providers — same windows, same dock.
